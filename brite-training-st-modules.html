Restructured. ST is the second role (after JT) to apply the BC-6290 pattern — lossless at-home consolidation, at-home overview → classroom detail with learning-confirmation quizzes, single-module Role Orientation, and Phase-2 Day pages. Knowledge quizzes now follow ADR 0004: open-reference acknowledgments, 100% required, unlimited retakes — "passing is the record you agreed to follow the process." Dates are targets; gates are fixed; JT/ST training windows stagger.

Phase 2 — On-Site Training & Certification
3 days · ~early Sep, before the JT hiring wave · AM-led · 2 classroom Day pages + Day-3 physical certs
Day 1 — Crew Leadership Classroom Day page · 6 LC quizzes
  • Daily Workflow Walkthrough — narrate your day; AM adds territory specifics
  • Documentation Standards — produce a full service report (photos, walkthrough video, footage + strand counts, FSM)
  • Apps Hands-On — Crew App + BriteNow; assign tasks, FSM entry, geo clock-out
  • Customer Communication Role-Play — the 5 scenarios; AM plays the client
  • Management Best Practices — corrections, handle/escalate, Hard No List
  • Wiring Routing Workshop — the at-home technique taught here; identify discrete + efficient routes, then the routing assessment
Day 2 — Safety Enforcement Day page · 4 LC quizzes + attestation
  • You're the Competent Person — assess hazards, enforce PPE, stop-work authority
  • Pre-Task Hazard Assessment — run the site hazard walk with the crew
  • Enforcing Heights, Lifts & Vehicle — enforce the JT safety content across the crew
  • Leading Incident Response — you run the 3-tier flow as crew leader
Single-sourced: Day 2 references the JT Safety Classroom through the crew-leader enforcement lens — it does not re-list the 25 safety modules. Ends with the annual safety attestation (named + dated, for the insurance record).
Day 3 — Physical Certifications 6 stations · compliance critical
132' Extension Ladder — setup, climb, work at height, descend safely (AM observation)
2A-Frame Ladder (12') — setup, inspection, safe use (AM observation)
3Roof Access — ladder-to-roof transition, working on a roof, harness use, safe descent (AM observation)
4MEWP Written Knowledge Test — open-reference, 100%, unlimited retakes (knowledge acknowledgment) (TM / certified trainer)
5MEWP Practical Operation Test — demonstrated competency; emergency procedures shown, observed + signed (TM / certified trainer)
6Greenery Quality Station — wreath placement & anchoring · garland tension & routing · bow mounting (secure the pre-tied bow via its backside wires) (AM)
Greenery is new as a tested station at the ST rank — the ST owns client-facing closeout quality (JTs are only field-observed on it). Bows arrive pre-tied for shape; the skill is mounting via backside wires, not forming the bow.
Phase 2 Gate: both classroom days attended · all 10 LC quizzes passed (Day 1: 6 · Day 2: 4) · wiring routing assessment passed · annual safety attestation signed · ladder/roof/MEWP stations + greenery station signed off · MEWP recert documented · AM confirms readiness. Physical stations: coach + re-evaluate as needed (demonstrated gates). MEWP written: open-reference, retake to 100% — OSHA 1926.453 / ANSI A92.24 cap no retakes.
Phase 3 — Mentored Crew Leading
Event-driven · begins when first assigned a JT to lead (~Sep 28, rides on JT Phase 4) + early peak · AM observes
Not a fixed block. ST Phase 3 attaches to whenever the ST has a JT to train — naturally the JT Phase 4 mentoring window (~Sep 28) and into early peak. Cleared STs awaiting a crew help with field work, maintenance, or warehouse duties (staggered windows).
What's Being Evaluated 8 competencies
  • Independence — runs the job without needing AM direction
  • Crew direction — assigns work clearly; JTs know what to do
  • Customer engagement — door knock, scope review, professional communication
  • Ethical management — corrections respectful; no Hard No List violations
  • Documentation quality — photos, videos, service reports complete & accurate
  • Safety enforcement — maintains standards under production pressure
  • Wiring decisions — discrete + efficient routing on real properties
  • Daily cadence — morning meeting, inventory, first job by 9, geo clock-out
Phase 3 Gate (Crew Leading Evaluation): min 5 jobs led · crew direction confirmed · customer engagement independent · management aligned with protocol · documentation passes spot-check (≥3 jobs) · wiring routing discrete & efficient · daily workflow consistent · AM confirms readiness for independent leadership.
Phase 4 — Cleared: Independent Crew Leader
Early–mid peak · no AM ride-along · formal sign-off once mentoring reps are logged · Peak opens Oct 12
What's Being Evaluated 5 criteria
  • Independent leadership — runs full daily workflow without AM direction
  • Consistent documentation — every job: photos, video, counts, FSM
  • Crew productivity — sustainable pace; no corner-cutting, no dragging
  • JT development — JTs are improving, not just executing
  • Problem resolution — handles weather/equipment/scope per handle/escalate framework
Phase 4 Gate — Formal Sign-Off: independent crew leading sustained · documentation consistent · no safety incidents · JTs confirm clear direction · AM signs off → ST cleared as independent crew leader. Active through end of season including strike (Jan+). Eligible for production-based bonus tracking.

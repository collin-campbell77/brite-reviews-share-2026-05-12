Phase 3 — Mentored Crew Leading
5–7 days · Real job sites · ST leads · AM observes (rides along first week, then steps back)
What's Being Evaluated 8 competencies
  • Independence — runs the job without needing AM direction
  • Crew direction — assigns work clearly; JTs know what to do
  • Customer engagement — door knock, scope review, professional communication
  • Ethical management — corrections respectful; no Hard No List violations
  • Documentation quality — photos, videos, service reports complete & accurate
  • Safety enforcement — maintains standards under production pressure
  • Wiring decisions — discrete + efficient routing on real properties
  • Daily cadence — morning meeting, inventory, first job by 9, geo clock-out
Activities
  • Lead full install jobs from arrival to sign-off with a real crew (1–2 JTs)
  • Handle all customer engagement — door knocks, scope reviews, change orders, escalations
  • Direct JTs through installation tasks — assign work, correct technique, maintain pace
  • Complete all documentation to standard on every job
  • Make wiring routing decisions on real properties
  • Attend morning meetings with AM/TM — report on crew performance, raise concerns
  • Begin coaching JTs (preparation for ST's core multiplier function in JT Phase 4)
Phase 3 Gate (Crew Leading Evaluation): Min 5 jobs led · crew direction confirmed · customer engagement independent · management aligned with protocol · documentation passes spot-check (≥3 jobs) · wiring routing discrete & efficient · daily workflow consistent · AM confirms readiness for independent leadership. Up to 5 extra mentored days if not passing; 10 total → TM review.
Phase 4 — Cleared: Independent Crew Leader
1 week · Job sites · No AM ride-along · AM available via phone + morning meetings · Formal sign-off at end
What's Being Evaluated 5 criteria
  • Independent leadership — runs full daily workflow without AM direction
  • Consistent documentation — every job: photos, video, counts, FSM
  • Crew productivity — sustainable pace; no corner-cutting, no dragging
  • JT development — JTs are improving, not just executing
  • Problem resolution — handles weather/equipment/scope per handle/escalate framework
Phase 4 Gate — Formal Sign-Off: Full week independent · documentation consistent · no safety incidents · JTs confirm clear direction · AM signs off → ST cleared as independent crew leader. Active through end of season including strike (Jan+). Eligible for production-based bonus tracking.

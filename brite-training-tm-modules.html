Phase 3 — Abbreviated Safety Certification (~half day)
Utah HQ · warehouse + yard · Director of Field Operations or existing TM observes · light cert — TM is a workstation-based managing profile
Why abbreviated: the TM works primarily from a workstation and visits sites for quality checks and team building, not installs. The cert ensures they can safely walk a jobsite, recognize proper safety (to evaluate crews), drive between areas, and own incident reporting as the territory's responsible party.

No JT Base Classroom and no ST safety-enforcement block — the TM isn't certifying crew safety enforcement. Canonical safety content is referenced, not re-listed (single-source).

Internal Promotion (AM→TM) recert quiz only · no physical practicals
  • Already fully safety-certified from the AM role — certification carries forward
  • Recert quiz (open-reference, 100%, unlimited retakes) covers: incident reporting as territory owner · vehicle safety review · safety oversight on jobsite visits
ADR 0004 — quizzes are acknowledgments. The A-frame practical is closed-book, observed, and signed (a real competency station). The written/knowledge pieces are open-reference · 100% · unlimited retakes. Compliance rests on one consolidated named/dated TM Safety Attestation ("I, [name], on [date], confirm I understand and will follow these safety procedures") covering ladder + vehicle + incident — that is the record.
Phase 3 Gate: A-frame practical signed off · vehicle safety understood · incident-reporting chain understood (AM → TM → Director of Field Operations → insurance) · can identify unsafe practices on a jobsite · consolidated TM Safety Attestation signed + dated · Director of Field Operations or existing TM confirms.
Phase 4 — Territory Shadowing
Peer-TM exposure (remote-feasible) + assigned-territory orientation · management shadowing, not field · Director-approved evidence gate
Adapted for a remote role. The JT/ST "observed in the field" model doesn't map to a workstation-based territory manager. Peer-TM exposure still happens where feasible — including remotely (reviewing another TM's reporting cadence, pairing on an approval queue, riding along when co-located) — but the binding requirement is a Director-of-Field-Operations-approved evidence gate, not a completed ride-along. The TM reports to the Director, so the Director signs off each stage.

Phase 5 — Supervised Solo
Assigned territory (remote workstation + periodic area visits) · pre-season ramp-up · Director-approved gates · first major test
Pre-season ramp-up is the first major test: the TM gets the hardest challenge — building the team from scratch — as their first solo responsibility. By the end, the territory should be staffed, prepped, and ready. The Director of Field Operations shifts from training to coaching; check-ins decrease as independence grows.

Seasonal Calendar (Year-Round Role)
TM is year-round, not seasonal · cadence shifts by season

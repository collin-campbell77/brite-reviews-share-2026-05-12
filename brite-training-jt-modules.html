Phase 4 — Field Shadowing
2 days · Real job sites · 1:1 ST-paired · JT executes, ST corrects in real-time
What's Being Evaluated
  • Self-motivation — moves to next task without being told
  • Attention to detail — clean connections, work to standard, notices own mistakes
  • Safety discipline — Phase 2-3 protocols applied on live sites
  • Speed & efficiency — no unnecessary hesitation or confusion
  • Client property respect — stays in work areas, protects landscaping, clean exit
Activities
  • Execute full line items (roofline, trees, ground displays, power) under ST observation
  • Apply the job flow from Phase 2 on real properties
  • Handle real-world variables (roof pitch, tree shape, outlet placement)
  • Use Crew App and BriteNow in live job context
  • Participate in job closeout procedures
Phase 4 Gate: Minimum 3 full job sites without safety issues + ST written readiness assessment + AM confirms advancement.
Phase 5 — Cleared: 2-Man Crew Litmus
1 week · Real jobs · 2-man crew (JT + one other) · AM monitors, formal sign-off at end
What's Being Evaluated
  • Independence — starts tasks at all levels without being told how
  • Process knowledge — can walk through each install type start to finish
  • Physical execution — works independently while partner handles own assignments
  • Consistency — no drop-off across a full work week
Phase 5 Gate — Formal Sign-Off: Full week on a 2-man crew · crew partner confirms JT carried weight · no safety incidents · AM formally signs off → JT cleared as full crew member through end of install season (~Dec 12).

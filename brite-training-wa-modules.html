Expanding Responsibilities — readiness signals (not a checklist)
  • Early Week 2: solo next-day prep, solo cleanup, routine errands · Signal: prep consistently accurate without corrections at walkthrough
  • Mid Week 2: SW Air runs without ride-along, C9 spool prep, helping pick/pull orders · Signal: reads packing list and pulls correctly without checking
  • Late Week 2: full daily cadence independently, light rollers (if present), unexpected tasks (mid-day crew requests, LTL receiving) · Signal: runs the day without morning direction beyond schedule
Phase 4 Gate: 5 days operating independently · next-day prep consistently accurate (correct SKUs, quantities, sectioned by installer) · workspace clean without reminders · end-of-day walkthroughs show decreasing corrections · shipping/logistics handled correctly · Tony (or TM/AM) confirms readiness for clearance.
Phase 5 — Cleared (End of Week 2)
Formal sign-off · 3 practical demonstration stations · Tony / TM / AM · Cleared WA = full team member through end of season
Practical Demonstration Stations 3 stations · pass/fail · highest-consequence tasks
1
Shelf retrieval — retrieve a specific product from a top shelf. Eval: proper A-frame setup (fully opened, not leaned), safe climb, controlled descent with product.
2
Light rolling — roll a returned strand correctly. Eval: female-end start, factory fold (~8" folds), last 3 bulbs unwrapped, male end tucked, no ripping/pulling on tangles.
3
Order pull — pull a complete order from a real packing list. Eval: correct SKUs, correct quantities, properly staged, two-copy system followed.
Form: wa-assessments/phase-5-clearance-stations.md.
Clearance Criteria — Tony's Standard
  • Work ethic — above bare minimum, proactive, does things without being asked
  • Punctuality — on time consistently; the season is built on reliability
  • Stamina — keeps pace day after day across an intense, long-hour, short-period season
  • Independence — runs the full daily cadence without direction beyond the schedule
  • Workspace — area clean every single day without reminders
  • Accuracy — next-day prep correct, packing lists accurate, shipping error-free
  • Safety — uses equipment correctly every time; no shortcuts on ladders, lifting, or vehicle loading
CLEARED — Warehouse Associate: all Phase 1–4 modules complete · safety cert on file · 3 practical stations passed · 2 weeks of consistent improving performance · end-of-day walkthroughs consistently clean · Tony (or TM/AM) formally signs off. Eligible for expanded duties: airport runs, C9 prep, light roller management, managing late-season hires.
Strike Season Addendum (January – ~April)
Returning WA refresher · Late-season hire compressed onboarding · Warehouse pivots outbound → inbound · Inventory count phase · See wa-in-season-protocol.md
Strike-Specific Adjustments
  • Returning WAs: ~3 days to full speed — paperwork check + technique refresher (light rolling, packing-list flow, scrap handling) rather than full track
  • Late-season new hires (2–3 typical): compressed onboarding — Phases 1+2 collapsed to Day 1 (warehouse walkthrough + safety cert), shadow + supervised solo abbreviated as inbound flow allows
  • Inbound flow: material recovery, sorting, scrap separation (minis vs C9), full inventory count in late strike
  • Tony's call: AM/TM coordinate; Tony remote-confirms clearance for late-season hires via FaceTime warehouse walk
In-season protocol source: operations/onboarding/wa-in-season-protocol.md. Mid-season replacements follow the same compressed Day-1 pattern as late-season hires.

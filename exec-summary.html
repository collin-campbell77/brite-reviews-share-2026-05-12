Why the re-sequence: The original Phase 1 (F10 offline + F09 quiz hints + F07) was optimized for "field-ready by Oct 1." But F09 has nothing to apply to (3 quizzes exist for 226 modules) and F10 is meaningless without internal alpha testing of the basics first. Foundation-first surfaces real issues before they compound. Total runway is ~5 months — enough to do this right.

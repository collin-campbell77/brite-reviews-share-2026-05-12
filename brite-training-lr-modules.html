LR is intentionally the simplest role — contractor structure, no remote pre-training, no JT Base Classroom, no formal phases/gates. Quality is assessed through direct observation by the WA (the primary trainer). Track is already condensed by design — minimal restructure needed for BC-6290.
Why LR Uses a Condensed Structure
Single-task contractor role · Standard 5-phase + JT Base Classroom framework doesn't apply
Why LR doesn't fit the standard framework:
  • Contractor, not W-2 — W-9, paid piece rate via Bill.com, no benefits or employee onboarding paperwork
  • Single-task role — rolling lights. No customer-facing work, no install technique, no field operations, no leadership
  • Hands-on only — no remote pre-training, no classroom, no quizzes, no certifications
  • WA-trained, WA-cleared — the WA is the primary trainer; AM coordinates; TM owns hiring pipeline
Trainer Responsibilities

Area Manager (AM)Coordinates hiring start dates, checks in on roller productivity, escalates issues to TM.
Territory Manager (TM)Owns hiring pipeline (Indeed posting, interviews, contractor agreements), sets territory rolling targets, monitors strand counts.

Section 2 — Hands-On Training (~3–4 hours)
Warehouse rolling stations · WA demonstrates each step, then roller practices with real-time correction · Method: WA does full cycle once, roller attempts each step
The core of LR training: WA works through each step of the rolling process, demonstrates proper technique, and watches the new roller practice until the motion is smooth. Mirrors the same walk-through-then-3–5-reps pattern used in QP/AM field training — but compressed into one warehouse session.
The Rolling Cycle 5 technique modules · observation-based
3Context: Our Work — what these strands become, why quality matters10m
4Step 1: Untangle — separate strands without damaging connectors. Observation pass/fail.30m
5Step 2: Rolling — folding technique, pinching every other bulb to alternate top/bottom. Bundles must be tight and even.60m
6Step 3: Tying the Knot — male + female ends tied properly, won't unravel in boxing. Observation.30m
7Step 4: Testing the Lights — every bundle plugged in + verified before stacking. Broken lights discarded.20m
Rolling is where most time is spent: The folding technique — pinching every other bulb to alternate top and bottom — takes practice. WA should expect to correct hand positioning multiple times. Reference video in the Rolling module showing veteran roller's technique at full speed.
Color Sorting (during testing)
  • Roller sorts bundles by color appearance during testing
  • Aged lights may shine differently — keeping similar-looking bundles together ensures trees look symmetrical during installation
  • WA demonstrates what "aged" vs. "fresh" looks like
Section 3 — Quality Check & Cleared (~30 min)
Warehouse · WA observes 3–5 full cycles independently · No formal quiz or certification · WA reports readiness to AM
Independent Cycle Observation 3–5 full cycles · untangle → roll → tie → test

After practicing through Section 2, the WA watches the new roller complete 3–5 full cycles independently without assistance. Same 3–5 reps pattern as QP/AM physical training.

Quality Criteria 5 standards · what the WA is watching for
CriteriaWhat the WA Looks For
Bundle tightnessBundles are firm — won't come undone during boxing or transport
Knot securityMale and female ends tied properly, won't unravel
TestingEvery bundle plugged in and verified before stacking
SortingBroken lights discarded, aged lights grouped separately
PaceImproving toward 20 strands/hour by end of Day 1 (full-speed target: 35/hr after 3 days)
Clearance Decision no exam · WA judgment
  • If WA is confident roller can produce quality bundles without constant correction → cleared
  • If not → WA continues working alongside them on Day 2
  • Use lr-assessments/wa-observation-checklist.md to structure observation + record clearance decision (formalizes criteria, not an exam)
Scope Boundary — Light Rollers do NOT participate in counting or audit processes. Strand counting, Monday.com updates, and inventory reconciliation are WA responsibilities. Light Rollers roll, tie, test, and stack — that's it. See training/operations/light-roller-management/managing-the-light-counts.md.
CLEARED — Light Roller: 3–5 independent full cycles completed without assistance · all 5 quality criteria met · WA Observation Checklist signed · WA reports readiness to AM. Roller is on payroll and producing billable output.
